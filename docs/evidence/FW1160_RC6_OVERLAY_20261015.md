# FW 11.60: RC6 overlay visibility confirmed

On 2026-10-15 at 21:21 Moscow time, after receiving RC6, the user reported:
«Вот теперь счетчик fps появился».

The same message requests a slightly lower position and smaller font, and
reports 89 and 120 FPS while the game loads. No RC6 log or screenshot was
attached to this confirmation. It establishes overlay visibility on the
user's FW 11.60 setup, but does not establish full lifecycle coverage,
measurement accuracy in this game, or whether those loading rates are
transients or genuine unlocked loading-screen FPS.

RC7 retains RC6's injection/guard-write implementation, changes defaults
from font 24 / bottom margin 10 to font 22 / bottom margin 4, and adds a
presentation filter common to VideoOut/DCE/HEN. Five-second settling and a
three-sample startup median suppress early unstable numbers. Brief large
rises require three observations over two seconds. Sustained 120 FPS and
variable higher rates remain permitted, while drops and short recovery are
retained. PID/source changes and sampling loss restart settling.

The filter is numerical; it has no game-specific loading-state signal.
Hardware testing of the new UI/filter and broader FW 11.60 lifecycle checks
remain pending.
